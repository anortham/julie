## Machine service design written and reviewed

Design doc: `docs/plans/2026-09-09-machine-service-design.md`. Status: awaiting user review, not approved.

### WHAT
- One `julie-service` per machine. Three bindings over one request engine: Streamable HTTP MCP 2026-07-28, stdio shim, plain JSON `/api`.
- Two durable roots per checkout (`facts.sqlite`, `tantivy/`), one `registry.sqlite` per machine. Graph, snapshots, and vector scan live in memory. Derived indexes are deleted and rebuilt, never migrated.
- Worktrees: one store per checkout, seeded by copying fact rows from a registered sibling found via `git rev-parse --git-common-dir`. No shared family storage.
- Semantics: `julie-semantic-sidecar serve` as one child; vectors as a table in `facts.sqlite` with brute-force scan; model chosen by scorecard.
- Miller's ten-tool contract carries over. CT deferred with its shape fixed (verdict rows, graph-walk selection, deny hook on full-suite runs).
- Section 4 complexity rule is normative: coordination words stop work; budgets in section 12 gate CI; net-negative lines gates on phases 2 and 3.

### WHY
Miller's last month of work (WAL debt, reader retention, idle leaders, 12 stale views, 1.1 GB resolution history, 255 s resolves) all trace to many processes sharing durable mutable state. MCP 2026-07-28 removed sessions and server-initiated requests, so a per-session server has no remaining reason to exist.

### HOW
Brainstorm with user, then Codex gpt-5.5 high read-only review of the draft. Accepted most findings; rejected dropping telemetry log and the Ponytail aside. Verified `rmcp` 3.0.1 ships 2026-07-28 with `server/discover`; `usearch` crate exists but was deferred.
