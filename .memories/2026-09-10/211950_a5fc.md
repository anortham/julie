## Completed Task 4: Model Scorecard Finding for bge-small and qwen3-0.6b

- **Evaluated Models:** `bge-small-en-v1.5-f32` vs `qwen3-0.6b-f16` on refreshed 10-repo, 23-case public corpus.
- **Results:**
  - `bge-small`: Pure semantic top-5 91.3% (21/23), MRR 0.837, peak child VmRSS 357.4 MB, prepare 0.078s, model size 133.6 MB.
  - `qwen3`: Pure semantic top-5 26.1% (6/23), MRR 0.261, peak child VmRSS 4,759.2 MB, prepare 0.650s, model size 1.20 GB.
  - Outcomes: `bge-small` won 16 cases, `lexical` won 1, 6 ties.
- **Decision:** Retain `bge-small-en-v1.5-f32` as `DEFAULT_NATIVE_MODEL`.
- **Sidecar Note:** `CodeRankEmbed` is absent from `julie-semantic-sidecar/src/manifest.rs` pins at commit `9ed082ba511aa8b10c9e7b47110c3a4dd1e98d59`.
- **Infrastructure Fixes:** Added `active_pid: AtomicU32` in `NativeEmbeddingProvider` to avoid mutex contention on `/status`, tuned `EMBEDDING_BATCH_SIZE` to 50, batch timeout to 180s, probe budget to 2s.
