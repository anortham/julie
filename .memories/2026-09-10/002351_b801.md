## Phase 2 Task 4: continuation store and spillover_get deleted

### WHAT
Deleted the third SQLite database per checkout (`continuation_store.rs`, `continuation.rs`), the `spillover_get` tool, its CLI command, the `julie-tools` and `julie-context` spillover modules, and their tests. `ToolCatalog` lists 12 tools. A bounded result that dropped rows now ends with `Output truncated at <N> results; narrow the query or pass a smaller limit.` (`truncation_line` in `crates/julie-tools/src/shared.rs`). Docs and skills that named `spillover_get` were updated.

### WHY
Design section 5.3: results are bounded; no continuation tokens or durable continuation pages. Removes a durable root and the busy-timeout retry it carried.

### HOW
Parallel batch A with Task 5 under parallel-lead-commit; lead reviewed and committed after Task 5 landed. Net about -2400 lines.

### IMPACT
Callers that relied on spillover handles get the truncation line and narrow the query. `ToolContext::session_id` is now unused in production (follow-up). `.claude/skills` changed, so `cargo xtask sync-plugin` is due before release.
