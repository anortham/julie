## Task 1: Stdio child provider

Replaced the native sidecar broker client with a direct stdio child running `julie-semantic-sidecar serve`.

- **WHAT**: Replaced broker client and lifecycle with `SidecarChild` running NDJSON over piped stdin/stdout; deleted broker coordination files, locks, sockets, and libc dependency.
- **WHY**: Phase 4 architecture eliminates multi-process broker IPC in favor of one child per service.
- **HOW**: Implemented `SidecarChild` with reader thread and bounded channels; updated `NativeEmbeddingProvider` to spawn and manage child lifecycle and auto-restart on exit; deleted broker challenge tests and replaced with stdio mock tests.
- **IMPACT**: Unblocks Task 2 (one child per service). Cleanly passes all native_child tests and durable_roots assertions.
