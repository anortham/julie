## Task 2 fix round 1: qualified resolution, SqlQuery edges, row-id lookup

**WHAT**
- `resolve.rs`: a qualified target (`Foo::bar`, `a.b.leaf`) narrows leaf candidates to those whose parent symbol is the last qualifier segment or whose file path ends with the qualifier components (`search::hybrid` -> `search/hybrid.rs` or `search/hybrid/mod.rs`); leading `crate`/`self`/`Self`/`super` are dropped; no survivor drops the edge (so `std::collections::HashMap::new` never binds a same-file `new`). A `Call` identifier on the same line and symbol as a qualified relationship row defers to that row.
- `julie-facts/src/insert.rs`: pending relationship rows now store the structured target's `display_name` as `to_name` when the extractor recorded a namespace path (receiver-based targets keep the leaf). The qualifier was otherwise lost before reaching the store.
- `EdgeKind::SqlQuery` plus `sql_query_edges` (view/update/insert/delete/merge fact -> unique table-definition fact) in `graph/web_edges.rs`; scores weight 0.
- `Graph::symbol_by_row_id` backed by a map built at load.
- `call_path_web.rs` follows `SqlQuery` as `sql_query`; `fast_refs.rs` match arms include the new variant.

**WHY**
- Lead review of the tool ports found qualified identifier ties dropped edges, no SQL edge kind, and per-hit `symbols_in_path` scans.
