## Task 11 fix: source_bytes from facts store

### WHAT
`metrics_source_bytes_for_binding` and the metrics writer fallback now sum `blobs.byte_len` for paths in facts, then fall back to on-disk size. They no longer call `symbols.db` `get_total_file_sizes`.

### WHY
After a facts-only `manage_workspace index`, get_context telemetry recorded `source_bytes=0` because file sizes lived only in `symbols.db`.

### HOW
Query `paths JOIN blobs` on the checkout store for the requested binding. Disk size under the binding root fills gaps.

### IMPACT
`test_get_context_target_workspace_uses_requested_binding_for_metrics_attribution` is green (`source_bytes=125`, `workspace_id=target`).
