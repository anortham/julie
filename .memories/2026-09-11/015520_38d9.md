## Fix live client/session NULL on tool_calls

- **WHAT:** `McpAdapter::call_tool` now reads `context.meta` instead of `request.meta`.
- **WHY:** rmcp 3.0.1 moves `_meta` into `RequestContext` before dispatch, so `request.meta` is empty.
- **HOW:** `mcp_adapter_records_client_from_request_context_meta` sets julie client/session on the context and asserts the `tool_calls` row.
- **IMPACT:** Live shim and `/mcp` rows should now store client and client_session.
