## What

Added a focused verifier regression test and changed its subprocess helper to use temporary files instead of PIPE capture handles.

## Why

The Windows qualification timed out while the shim's background service remained alive. The candidate already explicitly redirected every spawned service stdio stream to `Stdio::null`, so the claimed product inheritance cause was not supported.

## How

The regression starts a parent that exits after launching a descendant that keeps inherited handles open. It requires the verifier helper to return promptly. The helper now launches commands with file-backed stdin/stdout/stderr and waits only for the direct process, so descendant-held capture handles cannot delay EOF.

## Impact

Archive qualification will fail or continue based on the direct command outcome rather than hanging behind an orphaned descendant. Native Windows validation remains required for platform proof.
