## Windows lock cleanup closes the shim before stopping service

### WHAT

The Windows executable-lock probe now closes the no-args shim's redirected stdin and waits for that process to exit before invoking `julie-server service stop`.

### WHY

The old cleanup order stopped the service while the live shim was still reading stdin. The shim could reconnect or restart the service, leaving `service.json` after a nominal stop.

### HOW

A focused contract assertion first failed because `service stop` preceded `StandardInput.Close`. The two existing cleanup blocks were reordered without adding behavior or dependencies; the exact contract then passed.

### IMPACT

The local NTFS lock test can now prove archive extraction while an old shim is running and deterministic service cleanup afterward. Lead will rerun the real packaged test in win-test after commit.
