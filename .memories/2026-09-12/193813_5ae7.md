## Plan 3B deterministic barrier repair

### WHAT

- Repaired the test-only cold-initialization entry barrier to use `Notify::notify_one` instead of `notify_waiters`.
- Made the cold/warm test deterministically schedule the initializer by observing its atomic attempt counter before awaiting the entry barrier, with a bounded watchdog wait.

### WHY

`notify_waiters` keeps no permit. If the initializer reached it before the test registered `entered().await`, the notification was lost and the deterministic test could hang.

### HOW

- Exact RED test failed with `initialization barrier notification was lost: Elapsed(())`.
- Replaced the notification with `notify_one`, which retains one permit, and the same exact test passed GREEN.

### IMPACT

The Plan 3B barrier is race-free without sleeps. Production runtime-slot behavior is unchanged; this repair affects only test instrumentation and its test.
