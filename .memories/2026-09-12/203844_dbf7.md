## Recorded Plan 3 runtime lifecycle decision

### WHAT

Added `docs/adr/ADR-0007-request-runtime-lifecycle-owner.md`.

### WHY

Plan 3 consolidated production lifecycle ownership and removed the obsolete workspace manager/lease alternative; this prevents a future parallel lifecycle path.

### HOW

The ADR makes `RequestEngine`/`RuntimeFactory` and its per-key `RuntimeSlot` authoritative. It records periodic idle retirement, shutdown draining, ordered watcher/mutation then embedding teardown, and durable index preservation.

### IMPACT

Future lifecycle changes extend the factory and service loop rather than adding a manager or leases. Empty slots remain lightweight coordination metadata after eviction.
