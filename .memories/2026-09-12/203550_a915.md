## WHAT

Implemented Plan 3C service-owned runtime lifecycle work: a cancellation-backed maintenance task periodically retires idle slots, and service shutdown joins maintenance before draining every loaded and unbound runtime through `teardown_loaded_workspace`.

## WHY

Idle retirement must be owned by the service rather than request waiters, and shutdown must join watchers and embedding writers before releasing runtime slots.

## HOW

`ServiceApp::serve` starts maintenance from its existing shutdown token, awaits graceful HTTP stop, cancels and joins maintenance, then calls `RuntimeFactory::shutdown_all`. The service-control test creates a real watched workspace and a notify-gated registered writer, proves shutdown takes the writer then blocks until release, and verifies watcher/runtime release.
