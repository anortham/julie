## Plan 3C lifetime inventory

### WHAT
Documented the runtime lifetime inventory in `docs/findings/revival-runtime-lifetime-inventory.md` and linked it from Plan 3C. The task report records the Julie-backed evidence.

### WHY
Later teardown and retention slices need a concrete owner and quiescence contract before they change watcher, embedding, store, or slot lifetime.

### HOW
Traced request dispatch through `RuntimeFactory::acquire`, watcher task ownership and drain, mutation-gate blocking work, embedding registry/cancellation, store snapshots/caches, replacement paths, and service shutdown. The factory slot remains the only lifecycle owner.

### IMPACT
The next implementation must move workspace/task handles out before joins, block same-key acquisition at the slot, preserve a joinable blocking embedding path, and only clear the populated slot after watcher, embeddings, and gate-owned writers quiesce.
