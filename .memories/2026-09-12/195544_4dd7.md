## Embedding cancel-and-join

### WHAT

Added `cancel_and_join_embedding_tasks` in `src/tools/workspace/indexing/embeddings.rs`, routed force reindex/refresh and startup repair through it, and added the deterministic blocking-writer test in the active workspace test suite.

### WHY

Previous force and startup paths aborted the outer Tokio task. That could leave its awaited `spawn_blocking` embedding writer running while reindexing or reopening proceeded.

### HOW

The helper removes task entries while holding `embedding_tasks`, releases the mutex, signals each cancellation flag, and awaits each outer handle without aborting it. The outer pipeline already awaits the blocking task, updates daemon vector metadata, and then finalizes its registry entry. The test blocks a real provider batch with a condition variable, proves cancellation cannot complete before release, then proves a new schedule begins only after the old writer completed.

### IMPACT

Force index, force refresh, and startup repair now share a quiescence boundary for embedding writers. Runtime-slot eviction, watcher teardown, maintenance, service shutdown, broad gates, and Windows validation remain owned by later Plan 3 work.
